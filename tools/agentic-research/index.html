Principles for (agentic) research

A session in five parts: why do research at all, how taste is trained, what an agent actually is, how to run one as a supervisor, and an audit for the next task you think of delegating. The page grew out of a talk; the examples marked as from the talk are the ones given there. The tools will be renamed next year; the principles were written for people.

Agent, on this page, means the same language model as in a chat window, given files, a computer and permission to act on your behalf; section 3 says what each addition changes. Ten minutes? Read the bold line and the “In practice” line under each heading, then do the audit in section 5. Pitched at principles, not tools. Where a tool appears (a hook, a rule, a skill, a test), it is an example of a principle, shown in full so you can see what one looks like and copy it.
