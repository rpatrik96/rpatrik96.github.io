A session in five parts: why do research at all, how taste is trained, what an agent actually is, how to run one as a supervisor, and an audit for the next task you think of delegating. The tools will be renamed next year; the principles were written for people.

Pitched at principles, not tools. Where a tool appears (a hook, a rule, a skill, a test), it is an example of a principle, shown in full so you can see what one looks like and copy it. Your answers and read marks stay in this browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
